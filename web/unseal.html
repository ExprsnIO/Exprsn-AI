Submit your share

Each share holder submits their own share, one at a time, on this page. The shares are checked here, kept only in this server's memory until enough have arrived, and never stored. Use the same server for every share (the instance is named above), or unseal with exprsn-ai vault:unseal.

The full text of one share as kms:escrow printed it.

Break-glass seal

Holders of enough escrow shares can seal the vault at once, without signing in, when the admin accounts cannot be trusted or a second platform admin cannot be reached. Every instance drops its keys and refuses anything that needs one until the shares unseal it again. Otherwise ask two platform admins to seal it from the Platform screen.

At least as many as the escrow's threshold.
