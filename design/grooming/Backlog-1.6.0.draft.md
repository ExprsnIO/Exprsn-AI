## Descriptions

- **B-42 Platform administration live screens: Overview, Jobs, Storage, Configuration, Social.** B-4202 to B-4207 (PR #42): the five admin screens made live, with their routes, the settings descriptor with database overrides under dual control (Q2) and the integrity job. The boards (B-4201) are done. Decision Q12 in design/platform-admin; 1.5.0 has no room at the 78-point pace.
- **B-44 Groups depth: subgroups and channels, discovery, location, trending, categories.** Subgroups or channels inside a group (8), recommendations and a discovery page (5), an optional location on groups and events with distance filters (5), trending groups by job (3). Exprsn-AI groups are flat today. Plus a tenant-managed category list (3, decision Q6).
- **B-49 Quote posts and per-post visibility.** Quote a post with a comment (the platform's quotes), and public, workspace or unlisted visibility per post alongside its label.
- **B-46 Blob deduplication by content hash.** Reference-counted blobs so identical uploads share storage, within a tenant's sealed-key boundary only; the Storage screen shows savings.
- **B-48 Vault extras: secret sharing, MongoDB leases, access anomalies.** Share a KV secret with a principal under the path policies (3), a MongoDB engine for dynamic credentials (5), anomaly detection on reveals (new address, odd hour, burst) raising a flag (8). The platform's Vault had all three.
- **B-50 Capability tokens: resource-scoped, use-limited.** One mechanism for the platform's CA tokens: a token bound to a URL, DID or content id with a permission set, time or use expiry, issued and revoked from Settings and Identity; share links and scoped API keys become cases of it.
- **B-45 Tenant provisioning templates.** Create a tenant from a template (workspaces, roles, profiles, a zone, an issuer) from the Tenants screen and the CLI; the platform's enterprise, team and personal types. Decision Q11.
- **B-47 Held values on public forms are queued, not refused.** A public form value the user-input guardrail would hold is refused today (B-2205); queue it as a held submission for review like posts.
- **B-51 Release 1.6.0.** Version, CHANGELOG, docs and known-gaps sections for 1.6.0.
