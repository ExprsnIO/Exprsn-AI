Cloud spend: estimates, budgets, showback and anomalies

The screen #/finops (sidebar "Cloud spend") brings every cost the cloud work creates into one place: the estimate before a plan is applied, the actual bill ingested from each provider, budgets with alerts and a hard stop, showback per workspace and tenant, and the token cost of cloud models priced into usage records. It builds on the 1.6 usage and cost analytics (B-74) and the 1.1 price books, and never sends money anywhere.

Example data: September 2026 month to date, Monday 28 Sep 2026 09:40 UTC. Prices and totals are illustrative, not provider quotes.
