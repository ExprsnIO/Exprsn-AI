Part 9 · states for every cloud screen

States to design

Each cloud screen carries these states in its prototype module (applied from the command palette and by the smoke run). They reuse the console's empty, skeleton, problem and notice components, so a cloud failure looks like every other failure in the console: what happened, what still works, and what to do next, with the trace id.
