Cloud accounts and credentials

A cloud account is the unit of trust, scope and spend: one AWS account, Azure subscription, DigitalOcean team or Cloudflare account, with its credential mode, the regions it may use, the label ceiling and zone of each region, and its monthly budget. Platform accounts serve deployments and pools; tenant accounts serve one tenant's connections and model backends.

AWS trusts the Exprsn-AI issuer through an IAM OIDC provider; the worker calls AssumeRoleWithWebIdentity for one-hour sessions. Nothing secret is stored. 1
Vaulted static keys
An IAM user's access key in the vault as vault:cloud/aws-prod#access_key, sealed and never shown again; rotated automatically every 90 days by creating, verifying and deleting keys. 2

Notes

  1. 1Admin's choice per account. Federated is preselected where the provider offers it (B-10003 AWS, B-10004 Azure).
  2. 2Vaulted keys are tenant vault references with rotation schedules; the console shows last use and next rotation, never the value (B-10006).
  3. 3Disabled, not hidden. The radio is present and greyed with the reason, so an admin knows why there is no choice (B-10009).

Notes

  1. 1Three tiers map to the console permissions: Read (inventory, prices, billing), Deploy (create and change tagged resources), Admin (plus IAM for key rotation). The snippets change with the tier.
  2. 2Least privilege by tag. Every resource Exprsn-AI creates carries exprsn:managed, exprsn:account and exprsn:deployment; the policy refuses writes to anything else (B-10003, B-10004).
  3. 3Setup is copy and paste, with no Terraform or CloudFormation dependency; the role is the customer's to own.
